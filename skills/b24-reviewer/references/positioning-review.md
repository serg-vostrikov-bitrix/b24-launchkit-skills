# Positioning Review Profile

Main question: is this brief strong and safe enough to guide public copy?

Check:

- target audience is specific, not "all users" unless the evidence supports it;
- primary user problem is written in business language;
- main value proposition names the user, the job, and the practical outcome;
- key scenarios are selected for marketplace relevance instead of listing every capability;
- business benefits are direct consequences of confirmed behavior;
- important requirements and limits are visible before purchase or installation;
- `Do Not Claim` blocks likely overclaims, missing integrations, unsupported automation levels, security promises, and guarantees;
- search and FAQ intents reflect real buyer doubts and supported scenarios;
- recommended presentation angle is concrete enough to guide headline and first paragraph.
