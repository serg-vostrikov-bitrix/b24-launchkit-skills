# Final Claim Review Profile

Main question: can the target public artifact be published without unsupported claims?

Use this profile to review the target public artifact, usually the marketplace description. Use upstream manual and positioning brief only as evidence.

Check:

- the target artifact does not add capabilities absent from the upstream manual or positioning brief;
- the target artifact does not contradict `Do Not Claim`;
- limits and requirements did not disappear or become misleading in the target artifact;
- "can", "supports", and "designed for" were not upgraded into guarantees in the target artifact;
- claims about security, automation, data completeness, performance, synchronization, or business outcomes are directly supported.

Do not re-review the manual or positioning brief. Mention upstream artifacts only as evidence for or against claims in the target artifact.
