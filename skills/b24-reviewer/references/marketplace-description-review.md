# Marketplace Description Review Profile

Main question: does the text sell the right scenario without changing the facts?

Check:

- output contains only marketplace description: headline, body paragraphs, and FAQ;
- no introduction, rationale, source summary, review notes, SEO block, installation guide, support contacts, pricing notes, or second-language version unless explicitly requested;
- headline includes the app name, target scenario, and practical outcome;
- first paragraph shows the buyer's problem, the real alternative being avoided, and the after-state;
- features are translated into user benefits instead of dumped as raw entities;
- the text uses customer-first language: what the administrator, team, or business gets;
- the app is positioned against supported alternatives such as manual work, spreadsheets, custom development, duplicated data entry, unclear status, or lost context;
- tone is confident B2B product marketing: concrete, calm, useful, and slightly sales-oriented;
- no empty hype or AI writing patterns from the shared writing rules named in `SKILL.md`, and "powerful" or "unique" only when made specific immediately;
- requirements and limitations are visible and framed as usage boundaries;
- FAQ answers likely installation, fit, data coverage, repeat-run, error, and permission doubts.
