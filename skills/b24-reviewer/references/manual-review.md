# Manual Review Profile

Main question: can a user honestly understand what the app does, who it is for, how it is used, what result it gives, and what limits matter?

Check:

- confirmed facts only; no speculative features, guarantees, or marketing promises;
- user-facing meaning instead of implementation internals;
- clear roles, scenarios, setup expectations, outputs, permissions, errors, reports, statuses, and important limits;
- enough detail for downstream positioning without exposing source paths, class names, endpoint names, queue names, or database names;
- no conflict between product behavior and stated unsupported features.
